About these terms

These terms apply to purchases from, and services provided by, Bike & Butter, 491 Jurong West Avenue 1, Singapore 640491 (“we”, “us”). By ordering from us or booking a service, you agree to them. We may update them from time to time, and the version on this page at the time of your order applies.

Products and prices

All prices are in Singapore dollars (SGD). Some products are shown as “Enquire” and are quoted when you contact us. Prices and availability may change without notice, and we will confirm the final price before you pay.

We are the official Singapore distributor of 8LIEN, RSTRI and EVR. Product photos are for illustration, and colours may vary slightly.

Orders and payment

We accept PayNow and bank transfer, and online card payment through HitPay where online checkout is offered. We will send you payment details when your order or quote is confirmed.

Items that we order in from our suppliers, including RSTRI parts and EVR apparel, are ordered only after full payment has been received. RSTRI parts are made to order after you enquire and our mechanic has recommended the right set-up.

Delivery and collection

Collection from our workshop is free. We deliver within Singapore only. Orders of S$150 or more ship free, and orders below S$150 pay a flat delivery fee of S$15. We do not currently ship outside Singapore.

Items we order in for you typically take around 2 to 4 weeks. We will advise the estimated delivery date once your order has been placed with our supplier. Dates are estimates, and delays outside our control may occur.

Cancellations and changes

Once an order or quote has been confirmed and work or ordering has begun, we do not accept cancellations. If you cancel a workshop job after it has started, the full quoted amount remains payable as if the job had been completed. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to make a change, and we will do our best to help.

Returns and refunds

We do not offer returns as standard. If something is not right, contact us on WhatsApp and we will work with you to find the best solution.

Made-to-order items (RSTRI and EVR) and parts that have already been fitted cannot be returned. Other items in new, unused condition may be returned at our discretion. Where a return is agreed, any cost of collection or return delivery (for example by Lalamove) is borne by the customer.

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, please tell us as soon as possible. We will diagnose the issue and propose a solution case by case. Nothing in these terms affects your rights under Singapore consumer law.

Warranty

Products carry the manufacturer's warranty where one applies, and we will handle warranty claims with the manufacturer on your behalf. Warranty does not cover crash damage, or parts fitted by anyone other than us.

Workshop services

Our workshop focuses on servicing, repairs, upgrades and builds for road and triathlon bikes. Every job is quoted after you enquire and our mechanic has assessed the bike and the work and parts involved. We typically complete jobs in one to two weeks, although larger jobs may take longer.

For larger jobs such as full builds we usually take a deposit. For simpler jobs, payment is collected on completion. You may bring your own parts, or we can order them for you.

Our workshop is drop-off. If we arrange a Lalamove pickup or return for you, the cost and any risk of loss or damage in transit are borne by the bike owner. We provide a 1-year warranty on our workmanship. This does not cover crash damage or work carried out by anyone else.

Bikes for sale

Bikes sold through our Marketplace are sold as-is. You are welcome to view and test ride a bike before purchase. We do not take reservation deposits, and payment in full is collected once the deal is agreed.

If you would like us to list your bike, our mechanic will first inspect it in person. We agree a price with you, suggest a selling price, and, if you accept, list it on our website and Carousell. A small fee is payable to us when the bike sells.
