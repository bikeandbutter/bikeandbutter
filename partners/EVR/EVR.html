// materials & construction

Built to
Perform.

🇮🇹
Italian-Sourced Chamois

All EVR bibs and shorts use Italian-manufactured chamois pads. The range spans 70-density comfort pads for shorter rides through to 120-density performance foam and graphene-treated bio-based materials for 8-hour endurance.

◈
Full Moon & SiTiP Fabrics

EVR's premium jersey and bib ranges use Italian Full Moon fabric and SiTiP construction — both known for their four-way stretch, low-friction surface feel, and moisture management properties in hot conditions.

☀
UPF50+ Sun Protection

The Breakthrough jersey line carries UPF50+ sun protection — critical for Singapore's year-round UV exposure. Lycra antibacterial treatment keeps the fabric fresh across back-to-back training days.

♻
Bio-Based Materials

EVR's Double-Arrow 120 Eco pad uses bio-based perforated polyurethane foam. The CITY lifestyle tee is made from Sorona Agile — a bio-based fibre from DuPont. Performance without compromise on sourcing.
