Technology

Why Carbon Spokes.

↑
Stiffness-to-Weight

At 2g per spoke, carbon delivers a stiffness-to-weight ratio impossible with steel or alloy. Every watt goes to the road — no flex, no loss.

∞
Fatigue Resistance

Carbon doesn't accumulate stress fatigue over thousands of kilometres. Year three rides the same as day one.

≋
Aero Profile

Blade-thin spokes slice through air rather than disrupting it. At 40 km/h the drag reduction is measurable — and felt the moment you accelerate.

◈
Tubeless Ready

All AETHER rims are tubeless-compatible out of the box. Lower pressures, less rolling resistance, zero pinch flats.

⬡
T1100 + M40X Carbon Fusion

T1100 handles toughness across all conditions. M40X reinforces critical zones — spoke holes and rim walls — for sharp acceleration and cornering confidence. Tested to 90 joules: more than double the UCI standard.

What makes L-Series different

No Paint. No Excuses.

◻
Coating-Free Finish

No paint, no lacquer. The L-series exposes raw carbon — saving weight and giving each wheelset a distinctly honest, industrial look that stands out on any build.

↔
25mm Internal Width

All three builds run 25mm internal — optimised for 28–32mm tyres. Better air volume, improved cornering confidence, lower optimal pressures without sacrificing any stiffness.

⚙
45T Star Ratchet Hub

45T star ratchet engagement. Instant power transfer with near-zero dead band. You put the power in — it goes straight out.

◈
TPI Ceramic Bearings

Lower rolling resistance, higher durability. Ceramic bearings spin with noticeably less drag than steel — measurable in watts, felt in every pedal stroke.

Race engineering

Built for the Bike Leg.

◉
T1000 Carbon

T1000 is the highest-grade carbon in commercial production. Lighter, stiffer, and more impact-resistant than T700 or T800. The Aero X8 uses it throughout the disc structure.

⬛
Full Disc Advantage

A full disc eliminates spoke drag entirely. In TT and triathlon conditions, a rear disc is one of the single highest-return aero upgrades available — and at 950g it costs you nothing in weight.

↑
Sustained Aero

Disc wheels become more efficient the faster you ride. At triathlon pace — typically 35–45 km/h — the Aero X8 is in its element. Every watt saved on a 90km bike leg adds up.

◈
TPI Ceramic Bearings
