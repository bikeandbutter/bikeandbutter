The box

Built for race travel.

Size and weight

130 × 49 × 100 cm. Empty weight is 16–17 kg. Allow at least 25 kg all-in for a road bike and 30 kg for a triathlon bike, and check your airline's limits.

Fit

Made for tri bikes and fits road bikes too. Larger bikes may need the TT extensions or seatpost adjusted. Not sure? Message us your frame size and we will tell you.

Included

Two TSA-approved locks. No deposit. Cosmetic scratches are fine. If the box is damaged in transit, we help you claim from the airline.

Packing service

Unsure how to pack? Bring the bike over and we will do it for $50. Brake blocks go in, rotors come off if you want, and we add foam or bubble wrap where needed.

Collection and return

Collect and return at 491 Jurong West Avenue 1. Make sure your car or ride can take a 130 × 49 × 100 cm box. We confirm the time with you on WhatsApp.

Damage to your bike

For any damage to your bike or the box during transport, please make a claim with the airline. Boxes are also for sale, ask us.

Questions

Good to know.

How is the price worked out?

$10 per day per box, counting the day you collect it through to the day you return it. There is a minimum charge of $50 per box, so rentals of up to 5 days cost $50. The calendar above calculates it for you.

Do I pay when I book?

No. You send a request, we confirm availability with you on WhatsApp and send the PayNow details. Your dates are locked in once we confirm and receive payment.

Can I extend my rental?

Usually, yes. Message us as early as you can and we will check whether the box is free for the extra days.

What if my bike is damaged in transit?

Please make a claim with the airline for any damage to your bike or the box. If the box is broken, we help you with the claim. There is no deposit and cosmetic scratches are acceptable.

Can I rent more than one box?

Yes. We have several boxes. The calendar shows how many are free on each day, and you can choose the number of boxes after picking your dates.

Can you deliver the box to me?

Yes. Choose delivery and/or collection when you book, for $30 per way (flat rate within Singapore, up to 2 boxes). Otherwise self-collect and return at our workshop for free.

Can I buy a box instead?

Boxes are for sale too. Contact us for the current price.
