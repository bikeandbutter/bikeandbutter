How it works

Simple, and in writing.

  1. Book or message usPick a slot on the booking page or WhatsApp us. Tell us the bike and what it needs.
  2. We quoteEvery job is quoted after our mechanic looks at the bike's condition and the work and parts involved. Larger jobs usually need a deposit.
  3. Drop off at the workshopBring the bike to Jurong West. Can't make it? We can arrange a Lalamove pickup, at the owner's cost.
  4. Collect when readyMost jobs take one to two weeks. We let you know when it is ready, and you pay on completion.
