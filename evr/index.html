The Brand

Built for riders who
refuse to compromise.

EVR — Elevation, Velocity, Revolution — designs performance cycling apparel around Italian-sourced chamois pads and technical fabrics engineered for real heat and real distance. Bike & Butter brings the range to Singapore as its official distributor, backing a brand still finding its footing here because we believe riders deserve a serious alternative.

Materials

Details that hold up
to distance.

Italian Chamois

Pads sourced from Italian manufacturers, spanning entry-level comfort foam through to graphene-treated bio-based options rated for 8-hour endurance rides.

Technical Fabrics

Full Moon and SiTiP fabric construction — four-way stretch, low-friction surface feel, built for Singapore's heat and humidity.

UPF50+ Protection

Sun protection built into the Breakthrough jersey line, with antibacterial treatment to keep fabric fresh across back-to-back training days.
