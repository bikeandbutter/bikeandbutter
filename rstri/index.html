Engineering

Built to spec.

01

CNC precision machining

Every component is machined from billet aluminium to ±0.1mm tolerance. No casting, no injection moulding.

02

Bike-specific geometry

Each adaptor is designed for one specific fork geometry, not a universal compromise. Direct-mount fit means zero shimming.

03

Hard anodised finish

Matte black hard anodising is corrosion resistant, scratch resistant and dimensionally stable.

04

Hardware included

Every component ships with its mounting hardware, with an install guide and torque specs.

Why bike-specific adaptors make a difference

Universal aerobar adaptors create a chain of compromises (shimming, offset spacers, misaligned cable ports) that introduce flex, add weight and create fit uncertainty. RSTRI's approach is different: the fit is structurally correct from the first ride and the stack height is exactly what your fit demands, not what a universal adaptor happens to allow. For athletes racing on measured positions, this is a foundational upgrade.
