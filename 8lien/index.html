Technology

Why carbon spokes.

01

Stiffness-to-weight

At 2g per spoke, Gen 4 carbon spokes deliver a stiffness-to-weight ratio impossible with steel or alloy. Every watt goes to the road.

02

Fatigue resistance

Carbon doesn't accumulate stress fatigue over thousands of kilometres. Year three rides the same as day one.

03

Aero profile

Blade-thin spokes slice through air rather than disrupting it. The drag reduction is measurable at 40 km/h.

04

T1100 + M40X carbon fusion

T1100 handles toughness across conditions, while M40X reinforces the spoke holes and rim walls. Tested to 90 joules, more than double the UCI standard.

05

45T star ratchet and ceramic bearings

Instant power transfer with near-zero dead band, and TPI ceramic bearings for lower rolling resistance.

06

Tubeless ready

Lower pressures, less rolling resistance and fewer pinch flats, out of the box.
